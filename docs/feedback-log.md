### November 2026 · Business owner (first interview)

**Context:** phone interview, about 31 minutes. See the [idea](idea.md).

**What they said:**
- Their biggest problem as the owner is credit: collecting money customers owe.
- Face-to-face selling and word of mouth matter more than digital marketing.
- Automated daily messages to customers get ignored or arrive at bad times.

**What we learned:**
- Credit tracking is a real, painful problem that software alone can solve.
- Any reminder feature should keep the owner in control rather than spamming customers.

**What we'll do next:**
- [x] Build a prototype credit tracker with AI-written reminders the owner reviews before sending
- [ ] Factory visit: find out how credit is tracked today and get feedback on the prototype
- [ ] Show the prototype to the instructor and record their feedback here
